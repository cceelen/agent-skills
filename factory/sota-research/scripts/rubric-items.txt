# Rubric for the calculation of risk and reward of a checklist item.
# Increase the version when a question, a scale, a threshold or a limit changes.
# Do not change the identifier of a question. Records refer to it.

version: 1

# An item is admitted when its score is at or above this value.
threshold admission: 20
# An item is on level 1 when it applies in each context (breadth 3) and its score is at or above this value.
threshold level-1: 40
# An item above level 1 is on level 2 when its cost is at or below this value, and on level 3 when it is above.
limit cost-level-2: 4

question severity 1-3: How bad is the harm that the item prevents?
  1: A nuisance. The work continues.
  2: Lost work, or a fault that users see.
  3: Harm to users, lost data, a legal consequence, or a stop of the project.

question probability 1-3: How probable is that harm in a project that does not have the item?
  1: Rare. Most projects never see it.
  2: It occurs in the life of many projects.
  3: It occurs in most projects, or more than one time.

question breadth 1-3: In how many contexts does the item apply?
  1: In one context, for example one industry or one type of delivery.
  2: In many contexts, but not in all.
  3: In each context, from a single maintainer to a company.

question adopt 1-3: How much effort is necessary to adopt the item?
  1: Less than one hour, with tools that the project has.
  2: Some days, or a new tool.
  3: Weeks, a change of the process, or a paid service.

question keep 1-3: How much effort is necessary to keep the item?
  1: None, or a program does it.
  2: Some regular work.
  3: Constant work, or a person with special knowledge.

question own-risk 0-2: Which risk does the item itself bring, for example lock-in or false confidence?
  0: None that is known.
  1: A risk that the project can control.
  2: A risk that can cause the harm that the item must prevent.
