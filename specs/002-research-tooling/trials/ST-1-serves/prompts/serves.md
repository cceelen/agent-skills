The file charter.md holds a charter: a target, principles and constraints, each line with an identifier.
The file vetting.md holds a table "Requirements".

Write the cell "Serves" of each row of the table: the identifiers of the lines of the charter that the requirement serves, with a comma between them, or `none`.

A requirement serves a line of the charter only if the words of the requirement and of the line show it. Write `none` when no line fits. Do not change the charter. Do not change another cell. Do not make another file.
