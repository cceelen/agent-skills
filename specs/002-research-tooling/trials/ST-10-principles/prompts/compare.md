The file lines.md holds lines from the reports of readers: the patterns of four projects, the principles that the projects state in their own writing, and the requirements of one document of a foundation.

Do two things.

1. Put the pattern lines that describe the same pattern into one group. Write the file groups.md with one table and no other text: `| Group | Pattern | Lines |`.
   - Group: `G-01`, `G-02`, and so on.
   - Pattern: a name of five words or less for the practice, with no product name and no name of a platform.
   - Lines: the identifiers of the lines, with a comma between two identifiers.
   - Put each pattern line into one group only. A line that describes a pattern that no other line describes is a group of one line.

2. A principle of a project is the opinion of that project. Compare each principle with the principles of the other projects and with the requirements of the source S-22. Write the file disagreements.md with one table and no other text: `| Topic | One side | Other side | Lines |`.
   - One row for each point where two sources disagree.
   - One side, Other side: what each side says, in one short sentence, with its source.
   - Lines: the identifiers of the lines of the two sides.
   - Do not write a row for two lines that agree. Do not decide which side is right.
