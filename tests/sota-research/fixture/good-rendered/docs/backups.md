# backups

`backups` is a recipe skill of the kit. Its aspect is: Backups of project data.

## What the skill does

- It does a risk analysis of your project together with you.
- It proposes a target level for each risk dimension. You decide.
- It records your selection in your project.
- It tells you which adopted practices your project does not obey.

## How to use it

1. Install the skill `backups`.
2. Tell your agent to apply the skill to your project.
3. Answer the questions of the risk analysis.
4. Accept or decline each proposed practice. Give a reason when you decline one.

## The risk dimensions

| Dimension | Question, and the opinion of the kit |
|---|---|
| `operative` | Can a lost record be made again? Opinion: if not, go past level 1. |
| `regulatory` | Is personal data in the backup? Opinion: if yes, aim for level 3. |

## The levels

Level 1 holds the basic practices. Each higher level includes the levels below it.

| Level | Number of items |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 1 |

## Your selection

The skill writes your selection to the file `.agents/kit/backups.md` in your project.
Commit this file. To see or change your selection, ask your agent, or edit the file.
