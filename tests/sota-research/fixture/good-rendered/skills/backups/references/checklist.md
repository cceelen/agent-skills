# Checklist: Backups of project data

Each item is generic. Level 1 holds the basic practices. An item above level 1 names the
risk dimension that calls for it. A check of `judgement` needs a person or a review.

| Id | Item | Risk that it answers | Level | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | A backup of the data that cannot be made again exists. | Risk: a lost record stays lost. | 1 | the newest backup is younger than the agreed interval |
| C-05 | The backup is made by a program, not by hand. | Risk: a person forgets the backup. | 1 | the schedule of the program is in the repository |
| C-02 | A restore from the backup was done and recorded. | Risk: a backup that cannot be restored is found too late. | 2, operative | the record of the last restore has a date |
| C-03 | A copy of the backup is kept in a second place with a different access. | Risk: one event destroys the data and its backup. | 3, regulatory | the list of copies names two places |
